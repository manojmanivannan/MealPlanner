03 · The 7 day accents — the signature

A disciplined rainbow week

The day colors are the one memorable thing; everything around them stays quiet. They are never used as text — only as dot, left bar, and chip fill — so the yellow that failed contrast as a full-card fill is structurally safe here. Solids clear 3:1 on the surface (AA non-text); chip labels are white at 4.5:1. The original pale pastels survive as *-soft tints for subtle backgrounds.

07 · Elevation

One subtle shadow — or a border

Claymorphism (nested inset + outer shadows) is gone. A card is either flat with a hairline border, border-only with a stronger edge, or lifted by a single subtle shadow. The deeper tier is reserved for overlays.

Flat
Hairline border, no shadow. Default resting state for cards.
Border-only
Stronger edge, still no shadow. For grouped or inset regions.
Shadow
The single subtle tier — raised cards, popovers, hover.
